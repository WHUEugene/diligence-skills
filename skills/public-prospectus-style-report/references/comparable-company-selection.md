# Comparable Company Selection

Use comparable prospectuses as format and benchmark evidence, not as target
facts. Select peers in this order:

1. **Product match**: same or adjacent product form, e.g. aluminum alloy
   precision die-casting, thermal structural parts, communication housings,
   automotive die-cast parts, optical-module thermal components.
2. **Process match**: similar manufacturing route, e.g. alloy formulation,
   melting, cold-chamber die-casting, machining, surface treatment, testing,
   assembly, mold development.
3. **Downstream match**: same customer certification logic, e.g.
   telecom-equipment, data center, optical module, NEV OEM/Tier 1, power
   electronics.
4. **Project/fundraising match**: comparable IPO fundraising or expansion
   projects with disclosed capex, equipment, capacity, construction period, and
   capacity absorption.
5. **Risk match**: similar risks such as aluminum price volatility, customer
   concentration, certification cycle, gross-margin decline, fixed-asset
   depreciation, and capacity digestion.

For each peer, record why it is comparable and what it can support:

| Field | Required note |
|---|---|
| Peer company | Name, ticker if available |
| Document | Prospectus, registration draft, annual report, project announcement |
| Comparable reason | Product/process/downstream/project/risk |
| Useful chapters | Business and technology, risk factors, fundraising use, MD&A |
| Limitation | What cannot be copied to the target |

For high-conductivity die-cast aluminum projects, preferred peers usually
include aluminum die-casting and structural-part companies before broader
thermal material companies.
