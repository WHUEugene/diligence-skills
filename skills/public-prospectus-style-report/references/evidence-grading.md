# Evidence Grading

Classify every claim before writing it into the report.

| Label | Meaning | Examples | Report wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public fact | Verified public source | Exchange filing, government bulletin, company announcement | State directly with source. |
| Project-side claim | Target/BP/interview only | BP revenue forecast, unverified performance metric, claimed customer | Use `项目方材料披露/测算/声称`; mark verification need. |
| Comparable benchmark | Fact about a peer company | Peer prospectus margin, risk factor, fundraising project | Use only as benchmark; never transfer to target. |
| Inference | Analyst conclusion from sources | Fit with industry trend, risk judgment | State as judgment and name assumptions. |
| Missing evidence | Required but unavailable | Customer order, audit statement, third-party test, legal ownership | Put in relevant module and final verification table. |

Hard rules:

- Never convert a project-side claim into a public fact.
- Never copy a peer company's customers, margins, patents, or orders as target
  facts.
- Do not state audited financials without audited statements.
- Use explicit caveats for performance metrics unless test reports are present.
- Put unresolved gaps in `资料来源与待核验事项`; do not hide them in prose.
