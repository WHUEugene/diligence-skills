# Public Data Fit Map

Use this table to decide what a public-source four-module report can safely
fill.

| Module / topic | Public Data Fit | Typical Sources | Required Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 行业情况: 行业定义、产业链、下游需求、竞争格局、行业风险 | High | comparable prospectuses, annual reports, official policy/statistics, industry associations | Industry facts do not prove target competitiveness. |
| 项目业务情况: 项目基本情况 | Medium-low | BP, company registry, official site, government project pages | Equity, control, project-company rights, and team facts need internal documents. |
| 项目业务情况: 产品方案 | Medium | BP, official product pages, comparable prospectuses, patents | Product mix, volume, pricing, and customer fit require target evidence. |
| 项目业务情况: 技术工艺 | Medium-low | BP, patents, test reports if available, comparable prospectuses | Target-specific performance claims need third-party testing and production records. |
| 项目业务情况: 投资规模 | Medium | BP, government project pages, equipment quotes if provided, comparable fundraising projects | Capex, land, equipment, construction, and financing require quotes/contracts/approvals. |
| 项目业务情况: 财务预测 | Low | BP financial model, comparable annual reports/prospectuses | Do not present BP forecasts as audited or achieved financials. |
| 项目在行业环境中的优劣势: 潜在优势 | Medium | industry facts, BP, comparable prospectuses, patents/tests if available | Advantages are analytical judgments unless proven by target evidence. |
| 项目在行业环境中的优劣势: 主要短板 | High | BP gaps, missing evidence, comparable risk factors, public records | Risk relevance is judgment, not proof the risk has occurred. |
| 项目在行业环境中的优劣势: 可比公司对标 | High for peers, medium for target comparison | peer prospectuses, annual reports, exchange filings | Peer facts are benchmarks only; never copy them as target facts. |
| 投资方案与风控措施 | Medium | BP, legal/finance assumptions, comparable fundraising project disclosures, risk factors | Investment terms are recommendations and must become legal documents before closing. |
| 资料来源与待核验事项 | High | source registry, audit outputs, missing-evidence table | This is evidence plumbing, not a business-analysis module. |

## Evidence Labels

Use labels inline:

- `[公开事实]`
- `[项目方口径]`
- `[可比公司参照]`
- `[分析判断]`
- `[待核验]`

Every target-specific claim should carry one of these labels.
